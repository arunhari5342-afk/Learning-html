rich dad poor dad

robert t. kiyosaki

Rich Dad Poor Dad is a book written by Robert T. Kiyosaki that advocates the importance of financial literacy, financial education, and building wealth through investing in assets, real estate, and entrepreneurship. The book contrasts the financial philosophies of Kiyosaki's two "dads" - his biological father (the "poor dad") and the father of his best friend (the "rich dad") - to illustrate the differences in mindset and approach to money management.
